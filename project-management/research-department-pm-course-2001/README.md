# Project Management Course for a Research Department

**Format:** Two-part course  
**Instructor:** Eric White, through Theory and Practice, my consulting practice at the time  
**Original date:** 2001  
**Audience:** About 30 people in a university neurology department  
**Status:** Archived  
**Source files:** None on this shelf

## What This Was

The department had grown to the point where managing its existing and new research projects was becoming a serious problem. I designed and wrote a project management course for them using Microsoft Project. I taught it in two parts to about 30 people.

That's all the record holds. It comes from a résumé I wrote in 2008 and from my own project register.

## What Is Not Here

The course files. I haven't found them, and I am not going to rebuild the outline from memory and call it what was taught in 2001.
